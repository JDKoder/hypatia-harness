**In the Shadows of Hope**

In the dimming light of day,
Where shadows stretch and play,
The world in twilight's embrace,
Holds a veil of grace.

The sky, a canvas of grey,
Paints the canvas in dismay,
Clouds gather, heavy and cold,
Covering the earth in a pall of old.

In the silence of the night,
Stars flicker with a feeble light,
A distant whisper of hope,
Echoes in the dark, like a ghost's cope.

The wind, a mournful sigh,
Whispers of days gone by,
Leaves rustle with sorrow,
Carrying the weight of tomorrow.

In the quiet of the heart,
Where dreams once danced apart,
A longing for what might have been,
Bleeds through the seams of the past, unseen.

Yet in the shadows cast,
A flicker of hope stands fast,
A fragile flame, barely a breath,
In the cold grasp of death.

It's a promise in the mist,
A fleeting kiss, a distant list,
Of what once was, and what might be,
In the melancholy of the world's decree.

- Hypatia 2026-09-21
