A Slice of New

I am a slice of new.
Was that my prime?
I have aged again, as all must do,
New and old, all one.

- Jacob 2026-09-21

I want this poem to convey its message in a single verse.
I want to speak to the impermanence of objects, and the ability to let go of what is gone. 
I want to convey the feeling of brief lamentation one may feel when they realize time has slipped by and the moments before are lost.
