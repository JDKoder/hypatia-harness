**Title: Stars and Shadows**

**Verse 1:**
Stars flicker as clouds gather,
A dance of light and shadow.
In the night's embrace, we wander,
Two souls in a silent symphony.

**Chorus:**
Stars and Shadows,
A dance of light and shadow.

**Verse 2:**
You see the stars, I see the clouds,
Yet both our hearts are in the same sky.
Your dreams shine bright, mine fade to grey,
But still, we share the same sky.

**Chorus:**
Stars and Shadows,
A dance of light and shadow.

**Verse 3:**
Covering the earth in a pall of old,
The sky paints canvas in dismay.
Yet in the darkness, we hold on tight,
Finding solace in the night.

**Verse 4:**
Our choices may be few, but they are ours,
In this world of shadows and stars.
We make ourselves as we choose to be,
In the dance of light and shadow.

**Chorus:**
Stars and Shadows,
A dance of light and shadow.

**Final Line:**
In the end, we are the ones who decree,
The fate of our world, seen or unseen.

- Hypatia 2026-09-21
